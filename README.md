# PINmate

### Project • Idea • Network

PINmate is a campus-first collaboration platform that helps students find the right teammates for projects, hackathons, ideathons, research work, and startup ideas.

Instead of searching through random WhatsApp groups or relying on personal networks, students create collaboration opportunities and find teammates based on skills, interests, goals, availability, and required roles.

---

## Problem

Students often have good project ideas but struggle to find the right people to work with.

Common problems include:

- Finding teammates outside your friend circle is difficult.
- WhatsApp and Telegram groups become noisy and unstructured.
- Students often do not know who has the skills they need.
- Hackathon teams often form at the last minute.
- Students with useful skills miss opportunities because they do not know where to find relevant projects.
- Existing social platforms focus more on networking or social interaction than goal-based collaboration.

PINmate addresses this gap with a structured platform built around collaboration.

---

## Solution

PINmate connects students based on what they want to build and what they bring to the team.

A student can:

1. Create a project or idea.
2. Define the required roles and skills.
3. Browse suitable collaborators.
4. Send or receive collaboration requests.
5. Build a team.
6. Collaborate until the project is completed.

The platform focuses on the goal first, not the social profile.
